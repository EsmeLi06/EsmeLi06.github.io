About

Curious by design.

My interests have always been all over the place. A small business on Taobao at 13, coaching junior golfers, fashion design, interning at a bank, publishing research on white-collar crime. For a long time I knew how to do a lot of things without knowing what I loved. The thread, I eventually realized, was problem solving, and the quiet kind of joy that comes from making something out of nothing.

That's how I landed on Linguistics and Computer Science with Math of Computation. Code became my way of communicating the ideas I'd been carrying around for years, and the field where my creativity could finally take a shape other people could feel.

I want to make things that reach people on a small, almost spiritual level — the way a Pop Mart figure gives someone a flicker of joy on an ordinary day. Useful, beautiful, and quietly human. That's the work I want to devote my time for.
